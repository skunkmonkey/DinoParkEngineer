# Curriculum Content implementation guide

The opening and first transfer content are exposed only through
`src/curriculum-content/public.ts`. It is declarative, exact-versioned content;
copy never decides behavior. `createOpeningCurriculumPackage()` supplies the
signed dawn package, and `validateCurriculumPackage()` validates it against an
explicit inventory of domain references, entity IDs, and approved runtime
assets.

## Opening contract

The package fixes the dawn scenario to seed `1601`, a closed pre-opening park,
a pausable tick-300 visitor deadline, one unassigned partially configured first
feeding, and a successful first outcome. The second enclosure's automatic
closer is visibly disabled. The maintenance knowledge record is represented by
an explicit unavailable route and is absent from the second job's Worker
Context. Reuse therefore points to one deterministic stabilized near miss with
zero injuries and fatalities.

The incident links expected, observed, consequence, immediate causal gap,
affected entities, a Trace, and the responsible Prompt. The minimum recovery
surface offers two valid approaches: route maintenance Context or revise the
instruction. It includes a zero-authoring-cost Eval, candidate, Review,
intentional Deployment, successful rerun, park-open outcome, expressive reward,
and a Handbook entry that is explicitly ineligible for Agent Context.

The first relevant Handbook entry unlocks from the experienced near miss, not
from park opening and never from a lesson-completion signal. Final success copy
states the concrete result: the reviewed route maintained containment and the
park opened safely for visitors.

## Five-minute authored run

`openingRun` declares six deterministic beats at cumulative targets of 45, 75,
130, 200, 265, and 300 seconds: assign, observe success, reuse/near miss,
diagnose/revise, Eval/review/deploy, and rerun/open. Pause time is excluded and
guidance carries no reward penalty. These authored targets support
instrumentation; only representative human baseline playtesting can satisfy the
approximately-five-minute acceptance criterion.

## Missing-Context transfer

The transfer fixture changes the surface problem to Bramble the Ankylosaurus in
Gamma enclosure with Gamma Gate maintenance reported by an enclosure log. The
same maintenance record is explicitly unavailable before routing and available
after routing. Both opening guidance records are disabled. Transfer success is
observable only after Context inspection, routing the Gamma record, and a
successful feeding rerun emit `event:transfer-context-routed` with no casualty.

An optional transfer-specific hint becomes available only after
`event:transfer-missing-context-observed`; it does not prescribe an action
before the player encounters the transfer boundary and does not reduce rewards.

## D2 asset handshake

The opening requires `assets:bundle-mvp-park@1.0.0` and these exact runtime
assets at `1.0.0`: operational-warning cue, herbivore dinosaur, dust effect,
grass-path environment, enclosure gate, dinosaur-plushie reward, park Worker,
park overview thumbnail, and park visitors. D3 does not read files or infer
availability from paths. D2 or integration code must supply those identities in
the validation inventory; a missing bundle or asset blocks the package with a
stable diagnostic.

## Validation

Validation covers boundary schemas, canonical fingerprint, exact class/schema
references, entity availability, unlock reachability and cycles, transfer and
playtest links, stable copy IDs, accessible guidance, asset readiness, Handbook
separation, and the exact golden chain:

1. first feeding succeeds;
2. the missing-context near miss is stabilized without casualty;
3. the free Eval passes;
4. the exact reviewed Deployment reruns successfully; and
5. the player opens the park intentionally.

Validation additionally rejects non-increasing or non-300-second authored
timing, abstract lesson/grade success copy, a transfer that reuses opening
species/enclosure identities or guidance, non-observable success, premature
assistance, and a Handbook entry that does not unlock from the incident.

The `/curriculum` browser route renders this validated package without making
copy authoritative. It exposes the six opening beats, concrete success text,
post-incident Handbook availability, and a keyboard-native Gamma transfer. The
transfer withholds opening guidance, makes optional help available only after
the missing route is observed, and retains the exact success event in a
persistent status message.
